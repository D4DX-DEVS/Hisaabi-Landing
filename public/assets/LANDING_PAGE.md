## Core Features

### 🕌 Prayer Times
- Real-time prayer tracking with countdown timer
- Location-based accurate prayer times using the Adhan library
- Support for all juristic schools: Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali
- Beautiful Adhan notifications
- Prayer Qada (missed prayer) tracking
- Home screen widget for quick prayer time glance

### 📖 Quran & Duas
- Daily Quran reading tracker (Juz-by-Juz, page-by-page)
- Verse memorization progress tracking
- Comprehensive Dua collection (morning, evening, after Salah)
- Audio recitations
- Bookmarking system for saved Duas
- Mushaf (digital Quran) screen

### 🎯 Daily Goals & Tracker
- Customizable worship targets
- Progress tracking with streaks
- Visual progress indicators
- Achievement system
- Daily, weekly, and monthly statistics
- Salah activity status tracking

### 🌙 Ramadan Features
- Ramadan countdown
- Fasting tracker
- Taraweeh prayer reminders
- Special Ramadan Duas and Adhkar

### 📿 Dhikr & Tasbeeh
- Digital Tasbeeh counter
- Shake-to-count feature
- Custom Dhikr goals
- 99 Names of Allah (Asmaul Husna)

### 🤖 AI Assistant
- AI-powered Islamic guidance
- Personalized recommendations
- Knowledge Q&A

### 🧭 Additional Features
- Qibla direction finder (compass-based)
- Zakat calculator
- Nearby mosques finder
- Islamic calendar with important dates
- Hadith collection with save functionality
- Islamic quotes
- Mecca live stream
- Groups & community features
